Privacy Policy
We respect your privacy. Here's exactly what we collect and why.
📋 What Information We Collect

When you use Café Fams' digital ordering system, we collect:

  • Name — to personalise your experience and identify your order
  • Phone number — to allow you to retrieve past orders
  • Table number — to deliver your order to the correct table
  • Order details — items ordered, quantity, and total amount
  • Payment information — processed securely by Razorpay; we never store card or UPI credentials
  • Location (GPS) — used only in real-time to verify you are physically present; never stored
  • Chat messages — used only to process your order
  • Feedback & ratings — if you voluntarily submit a review
🎯 How We Use Your Information
  • Processing and delivering your food order
  • Sending order status notifications
  • Allowing you to reorder using your phone number
  • Improving our menu and service based on feedback
  • Fraud prevention (GPS radius check)
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.
🔐 Data Storage & Security

Order data is securely logged in our private Google Sheets, accessible only to Café Fams management. Payments are handled by Razorpay (PCI-DSS compliant). Your GPS location is checked in real-time and never stored. Chat history lives only in your browser session and clears when you close the app.

📅 Data Retention

Order records are retained for a maximum of 90 days for accounting purposes, after which they are deleted. Feedback is retained in anonymised form to improve our service.

👤 Your Rights

You have the right to request a copy of your data, request deletion of your data, or withdraw consent at any time by contacting us at the address below.

🍪 Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser sessionStorage to temporarily save your table, name, and order details during your visit. This is automatically cleared when you close the app. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking.

☕ About Café Fams

Café Fams is a restaurant in Dhupguri, West Bengal, India. Our digital ordering system allows dine-in customers to browse the menu, place orders, and make payments from their table using a QR code.

This service is for dine-in customers only who are physically present at the restaurant. Remote ordering is not supported.
✅ Eligibility to Use
  • You must be physically present at Café Fams to place an order (verified via GPS)
  • You must provide accurate name and phone number
  • You must be 18 years or older to make payments
  • One session per table — multiple simultaneous sessions may cause order conflicts
🍽️ Ordering & Menu

Menu items and prices may change without notice. Item availability may vary daily. All prices include GST (5%). If there is a confusion in your order, use "Talk to Human" or call a waiter before preparation begins.

💳 Payments

Payments are processed via Razorpay. We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, and net banking. Cash is also accepted at the counter.

Café Fams does not store your payment credentials. All transactions are encrypted by Razorpay's secure payment infrastructure.
⚠️ Acceptable Use

You agree NOT to place fake or fraudulent orders, attempt to bypass geo-radius verification, use the AI chatbot for non-ordering purposes, or abuse the system in any way. Violations may result in order cancellation and access being blocked.

⚖️ Limitation of Liability

Café Fams shall not be held liable for delays due to high order volume, allergic reactions (please inform staff of allergies before ordering), or technical issues caused by third-party services (Razorpay, network providers).

❌ Order Cancellation

An order can be cancelled only before the kitchen changes status to "Preparing". Once preparation begins, cancellation is not possible. Use the "Cancel Order" option in the Orders tab or inform staff immediately.

🔄 How to Request a Refund
  1. Speak to a Café Fams staff member at the restaurant, or
  2. Email cafefams@gmail.com with your Order ID and reason
  3. Share your payment screenshot or Razorpay transaction ID
  4. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method
For payment failures or duplicate charges, refunds are handled automatically by Razorpay and reversed to your original payment method.
🚫 Non-Refundable Situations
  • Order already served and partially or fully consumed
  • Customer left the restaurant without raising a complaint
  • Dissatisfaction with spice level when ordered per menu description
  • Change of mind after food has been prepared
